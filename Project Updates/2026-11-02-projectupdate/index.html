Request for Proposals - Implementations

November 2, 2026

Lyrasis seeks one or more proposals to implement interoperability features that have been specified in Phase I of the CST Interoperability Project.

These specifications impact ArchiveSpace, DSpace, and VIVO, and involve the development of new code for ArchiveSpace and VIVO. This work is funded by Lyrasis through the CST Growth Fund.

The final deliverables will be submitted to the ArchivesSpace and VIVO communities for review and acceptance. Proposals may cover one, two, or all three of the following work packages and Lyrasis may choose not to fund all.

  • Work Package A: Bidirectional Linking between ArchivesSpace and DSpace
  • Work Package B: ArchivesSpace SWORD Deposit (DSpace as exemplar)
  • Work Package C: VIVO SWORD Deposit (DSpace as exemplar)

For full details and links to the specifications see the Request For Proposals (RFP) document.

Key dates:

  • November 2, 2026: RFP Release
  • November 16, 2026: Bidders Information Session (10am ET - register here)
  • November 23, 2026: Written questions due (5:00pm ET)
  • December 14, 2026: Answers published for all bidders
  • December 23, 2026: Proposals due (5:00pm ET)
  • January 18-30, 2027: Finalist interviews
  • February 15, 2027: Award notification
  • March 1, 2027: Work begins
  • December 31, 2027: All work packages complete (latest)

Please reach out to orghome at lyrasis.org with any questions!
